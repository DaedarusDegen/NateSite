DIY Installation Guide

Installed in hours.
No trades needed.

Our tiles click together by hand or with a rubber mallet — no glue, no curing time. Here's how to do it.

How to Install
5 simple steps
Follow these steps and most garages are done in a few hours. You can drive on the tiles the same day.
1

Measure your space

Measure the length and width of your garage floor in metres. Multiply the two together to get your total area in square metres. Each tile covers 0.16 m² (400 mm × 400 mm). Use our tile calculator on the home page to get an exact tile count.

💡

We recommend ordering an extra 5–10% to allow for cuts around walls, posts, or drains — especially if your layout includes cuts.

2

Choose your colour and pattern

Pick from 15 available colours in any combination. You can go single colour, checkerboard, border frame, racing stripes, or any custom design. Not sure what will look best? Contact us and we'll help you plan your layout before you order.

🎨

Check out our Patterns page for inspiration — designs are shown on a standard 6 m × 6 m double garage using a 15×15 tile layout.

3

Prepare your floor

Sweep or vacuum the floor to remove dirt, dust, and debris. The floor should be dry and reasonably flat. Garage Pro tiles work on both sealed and unsealed concrete. No adhesive or primer is needed — the tiles sit directly on the floor.

✅

If your floor has a large crack or significant uneven area, pack or sand it flat first. Small imperfections are fine — the tiles will bridge them.

4

Start from a corner, work outward

Begin at the back corner of your garage and work toward the door opening. Click the tiles together by hand or with a light tap from a rubber mallet — they should snap together firmly with no gaps. Work in rows, one at a time. Leave a small expansion gap of 5–10 mm at the walls to allow for temperature movement.

🔧

For tight spots or corners, a rubber mallet makes seating tiles easier. A standard jigsaw or drop saw cuts tiles cleanly for around posts, drains, or odd-shaped walls.

5

Finish with edge ramps and corner pieces

Once all tiles are down, snap edge ramps along the garage door opening and any exposed edges. Use corner pieces where two edge ramps meet. Edge ramps create a smooth, safe ramp for tyres to roll onto — no trip hazard, no lifting of tiles at the entry.

🏁

Edge ramps and corner pieces are available in Black, Grey, and Light Grey. Your garage is now ready to use — you can drive on it immediately.
